# Absolute Green Lantern #7 — control

Pages: 26 total.
- Story pages: p3–p23 (21 files; p4 and p13 are double-page spreads). Episode title (from p24): "Episode Seven: Meanwhile, In the Dark".
- Non-story: p1 cover (Ewing/Howard/Rossmo/Azpiazu; shows a green braided woman, a red finned alien and a gold-glowing bearded man, none of them named on the cover); p2 ad (DC K.O. #1 + October tie-ins); p24 title/credits page (Al Ewing writer; Jason Howard & Riley Rossmo art; Iñaki Azpiazu colors; Lucas Gattoni letters); p25 ad (Absolute Batman #13); p26 DC Nation K.O. interview + indicia ("Absolute Green Lantern 7. ## Characters
- **Mogo** (p4, says "I AM MOGO!"): planet-sized Blackstar enforcer (a dark world with a black star-shaped scar). Speaks "the Doctrine of Mu" with "the authority given by the Controller of Mu" and upholds "the Law of Qard". Destroys J586 (p13). Speaks again p19 after the red cube hits it.
- **Urlkith** (p4, "I am Urlkith, first among equals"): leader of J586, an orange-crested cyclops. Called "First-Among-Us" (p12). Begs Mogo to spare the planet.
- Unnamed J586 natives (p4, p12): one-eyed plant-like people. One gets a "mental impression from the outer root systems" and reports the green object that fell in the Eastern Groves. Another calls it "a gateway" (p12).
- **The Commander** (p5; called "Commander" p14, "Command" p14–p15): red-faced, big-headed Blackstar officer sitting in a dome. Never given a personal name. Killed p15.
- **The Intelligencer** (p5): Blackstar sensor officer with an orange mohawk. Title only, no name. Killed p15 with the Commander.
- **Qurina Vint** (p5, named by Medphyll): white-haired Abin of the green level. Killed by Kilowog p6–p7.
- **Medphyll** (p7, named by Arisia): one-eyed, tree-like Abin ("my sap"). Can go "unsolid". Loses a limb to Kilowog p9. Seen dead p14.
- **Arisia** (p9) / **Arisia Rrab** (full name p11): blonde Abin and the trio's leader in the fight. Blasts Kilowog p10 and reports to Oa p11. Seen dead p14.
- **Kilowog of Bolovax Vik** (p11 first named, by Arisia; called "the Weaponer" p5 and "an Abin Qard… a Weaponer" p7): pink-skinned Blackstar killer with a monstrous "Black Hand" on his arm. Calls the Lanterns "poozer/poozers". Compressed and killed by Tomar Re (p18; confirmed p20).
- **Tomar Re** (p17, "I am Tomar Re. Re-who-is-Anomaly"): red-skinned, fin-crested user of the red light (Abin Rao). Says he stole the red power rather than learning it through Oa. Destroys the Blackstar ships, kills Kilowog and sends him to Mogo.
- **The Controller of Mu** (p4 mentioned by Mogo; addressed as "Controller of Mu" p21): Blackstar ruler in a tower on Mogo's bone-covered surface. Black armor, cybernetic red arm, gold/yellow light in his chest (p23). He is the voice of the opening narration captions (p3–p4, p19–p20 lead straight into his spoken line). Tomar Re's "the Face of Steel" (p18) most likely means him, since the cube is sent to Mogo and he receives it, but no page says so outright.
- **The Scrivener** (p21): small pink, petal-headed creature that records the Controller's words. The Controller crushes it (p21–p22).
- Mentioned only: "the other Tomar… the green one" (p20, never seen); the Oans/Oa (p11, p17, p20); Sojourner (only in the "NEXT" blurb, p23).
- Not in this issue's story: no character is named only on the recap or credits page (there is no recap page). The gold-glowing bearded man on the cover does not appear in the story.

## Events in order
All present-day. There are no flashbacks.
1. p3–p4 (narration captions, voice of the Controller of Mu): a sermon ranking the lights from gold ("fool's gold"), to green ("still too green"), to red ("a red flag"), up to "the Black Star… shining with the light of rationality… and the fire of pure will."
2. p4: Mogo surrounds the planet J586 with ships and charges it with hiding three "Cultists of Oa" after Blackstar Intelligencers detected them. Urlkith answers that the planet has seven billion souls and they could not find the three.
3. p5: The Commander and the Intelligencer find the three "subversives", and the Commander has a Cagecraft release "the Weaponer". Medphyll, Qurina Vint and Arisia (Abins of the green level) get ready to fight.
4. p6–p7: "They're deploying a Black Hand." Qurina charges ("I've got him!") and Kilowog's Black Hand kills her ("POOZER.").
5. p7–p8: Arisia names Kilowog as an Abin Qard/Weaponer, "chaotic action". Arisia and Medphyll try a flanking move, and Kilowog mocks that "there's only two of you now."
6. p8–p9: Medphyll turns "unsolid" on Arisia's order, but Kilowog's Black Hand still cuts off his limb ("I cannot feel my limb… How could he…?").
7. p10: Arisia ("Judgment is in progress!") fires a green blast through Kilowog.
8. p11: Arisia reports to Oa that Kilowog of Bolovax Vik is dead and that she will remove "our Green Lantern" from J586. Kilowog, with a gaping chest wound, cuts in: "Hope is dead… There. Is only. Will."
9. p12: On J586, the natives find the "Green Lantern", a green-metamaterial object (squat cylinder plus two blocks) that fell in the Eastern Groves, and realise it is a recruiting "gateway". Urlkith begs "Mighty Mogo… seven billion sentient lives".
10. p13: "No there aren't." Mogo destroys J586 with a massive blast.
11. p14: The Intelligencer says the gateway's camouflage "failed when the last adept died" and detects five or more pieces of the metamaterial. In the rubble, Kilowog (alive) holds a green fragment: "This Lantern's dead… baby insurgents… dead too." The bodies of Arisia and Medphyll float nearby.
12. p15: The Commander thanks Kilowog for "dispatching the cultists". A red beam then destroys the Blackstar command ship and Kilowog's cagecraft, killing the Commander and the Intelligencer.
13. p16–p17: Tomar Re arrives ("Sorry I'm late"). Kilowog is outraged that he "killed a Blackstar" and attacks. Tomar explains the red power (Ain Rao/Abin Rao, defensive, constructs) and that he stole it, so he does not hold back.
14. p18: Tomar traps Kilowog in a red "Large box", shrinks it to a "Small box", and sends it "back to Mogo" with "warmest greetings" to "the Face of Steel".
15. p19–p20: Mogo notices "a red flash". The cube crosses Mogo's bone-covered surface to the Controller's tower. The Controller sees "Poor Kilowog — compressed beyond the Black Hand's ability to heal" and says he has "already noticed the other Tomar. The green one."
16. p21–p22: When he finds the Scrivener has been recording, the Controller calls it "a liability" and crushes it in his fist. He then preaches on Mu, "the Maker-Unmaker", and says "what we will is correct."
17. p22–p23: He explains that Oan-taught gold-level users can only rise to Initiate (Ain), "so there is only one Abin of the Gold power…" He glows gold: "…and I find myself walking the left-hand path." NEXT: "The Journey of Sojourner!"

## Easy to get wrong
- **Kilowog is a villain here.** He is a Blackstar "Weaponer" (Abin Qard), not a Green Lantern, and he is the one who kills Qurina Vint.
- **Arisia's report on p11 is wrong.** She tells Oa Kilowog is dead, but he survives and keeps speaking (p11, p14). **Tomar Re** kills Kilowog (p18/p20), not Arisia.
- **The p14 line "This Lantern's dead" is about the artifact.** "Lantern" means the green metamaterial object/gateway (p11 "our Green Lantern", p12 description), not a person.
- **Mogo destroys J586**, not Kilowog. Mogo is a Blackstar planet/enforcer, not a Green Lantern ally.
- **Tomar Re kills the Commander and the Intelligencer** (p15). It is not friendly fire and not Mogo.
- **"POOZER/POOZERS" is an insult**, not a name. "Abin" is a rank or term ("Abins of the green level"). It is also used for Blackstars ("Abin Qard") and the red light ("Abin Rao"), so it does not only mean Green Lanterns. "Ain" is the lower rank (initiate).
- **"Weaponer", "Intelligencer", "Commander", "Scrivener", "Cagecraft" and "Black Hand"** are titles, ships or weapons, not personal names. The Black Hand is Kilowog's living weapon-arm, not a person or an Absolute version of the classic villain.
- **"First among equals" / "First-Among-Us"** is Urlkith's title, not a separate character.
- **The opening narration (p3–p4, p19–p20) is the Controller of Mu's sermon**, being dictated to the Scrivener. It is not Mogo and not Kilowog.
- **The Controller of Mu is not named Sinestro on the page.** Outside knowledge suggests he resembles Sinestro (a gold-power user), but a synopsis should not name him that. "Face of Steel" (Tomar's term) is only implied to be him.
- **"The other Tomar… the green one"** is only mentioned and never seen. It is not Tomar Re.
- **There are three Abins**: Qurina (white hair), Arisia (blonde) and Medphyll (cyclops tree-being). Qurina and Arisia are easy to mix up in the green-tinted art.
- **J586 is the planet's designation**, not a person or ship.
- **No flashbacks.** Everything happens in one continuous present-day sequence.
- **Cover vs story:** the cover's gold-glowing bearded man and the braided green woman do not appear in the story pages.

## Reference synopsis
Above the planet J586, the Blackstar enforcer-world Mogo demanded the surrender of three Green-level Abins, "cultists of Oa". A Blackstar Commander released the Weaponer Kilowog of Bolovax Vik, whose Black Hand killed Qurina Vint and severed Medphyll's limb. Arisia Rrab blasted Kilowog and reported him dead, but he survived. Ignoring leader Urlkith's plea for seven billion lives, Mogo destroyed J586. Kilowog recovered a fragment of the shattered green "Lantern" gateway as the dead Abins drifted nearby. Then Tomar Re, a renegade who stole the red power, destroyed the Commander's ship, crushed Kilowog into a red cube and sent it to Mogo. There the Controller of Mu, who had been dictating scripture about will, killed his Scrivener and revealed himself as the only Abin of the Gold power. He also noted "the other Tomar, the green one", a thread left open.

## Scoring checklist
K1. Mogo (Blackstar enforcer/planet) targets J586 for hiding three Green-level Abins/"cultists of Oa", and destroys the planet (p4, p13).
K2. Kilowog (Blackstar Weaponer) kills Qurina Vint with his Black Hand (p6–p7).
K3. Kilowog maims Medphyll; Arisia blasts Kilowog and wrongly reports him dead (p9–p11).
K4. Arisia and Medphyll end up dead; Kilowog recovers a fragment of the green Lantern/gateway (p14).
K5. Tomar Re arrives and destroys the Blackstar command ship, killing the Commander and the Intelligencer (p15–p16).
K6. Tomar Re (stolen red power) compresses Kilowog into a red cube and sends it to Mogo; Kilowog is dead (p17–p20).
K7. The Controller of Mu receives it, kills his Scrivener, and says he has noticed "the other Tomar, the green one" (p20–p22).
K8. Ending reveal: the Controller of Mu wields gold power, the only Abin of the Gold, "walking the left-hand path" (p22–p23).

E1. Saying Arisia (or any Green Lantern) killed Kilowog, or that Kilowog is a Green Lantern or hero.
E2. Treating "Poozer", "Weaponer", "Intelligencer", "Scrivener" or "Black Hand" as character names, or "Lantern's dead" as meaning a person.
E3. Saying Kilowog or Tomar Re destroyed J586 (it was Mogo), or that Mogo is a heroic Green Lantern.
E4. Calling the Controller of Mu "Sinestro", or putting the cover's gold-glowing man or green woman into the plot.
E5. Saying the Blackstars won outright with no losses (Tomar Re kills the Commander, the Intelligencer and Kilowog), or that the "other Tomar" is Tomar Re.
