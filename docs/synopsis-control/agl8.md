# Absolute Green Lantern #8 — control

Pages: 31 total. Story pages: p03–p24 (22 pages). Non-story: p01 cover; p02 ad (Batman & Robin: Year One collection); p25 title/credits page ("Episode Eight: Sojourner", Al Ewing / Jahnoy Lindsay / Lucas Gattoni); p26–p29 Batman/Static: Beyond #1 preview; p30 ad (Justice League vs. Godzilla vs. Kong 2 #5); p31 DC Nation "Spotlight On Batman/Static: Beyond" interview + indicia.

Almost the whole issue is a flashback through Jo's life, from childhood to just before the present. Only p24 is marked as the present ("COAST CITY. NOW."). The flashback is broken up by four recurring color-tinted memory flashes, first shown together on p03: gray (Cameron: "It's all about that big zero inside you"), red (Dad: "You need restraint in this world"), green (crying adult Jo: "I just want to do the right thing"), and yellow/orange (young Jo in the car: "I don't understand anything").

## Characters
- **Sojourner "Jo" Mullein** — first named p04 ("Sojourner"); "Jo" first used p06. Protagonist. Shown as a child, then as an Evergreen PD officer, then in Coast City, and at the end glowing green.
- **Dad** (Jo's father) — shown from p03, called "Dad" on p04. His full name, **Thomas Mullein** (1961–2022, "A well-respected man"), appears only on his gravestone (p21). A strict, religious man who preaches "restraint" and defends the Hand family.
- **Harold** — named p05 by Dad ("That Harold..."). A neighborhood boy who says he will be a pilot (p05). Dad calls him a boy with "no willpower, no restraint." His surname is never given.
- Unnamed boy with water pistols (p05) who says "I'm gonna be a cop! Space cop!" Dad answers "Told you," which suggests this is Harold the next day, but the art does not confirm it. [unclear]
- **Chief Hand / Bill / Bill Hand** — first named p06 ("Chief Hand," "It's Bill. Always Bill"). Full name "Bill Hand" appears on p17. He is the chief of the Evergreen PD, has a gold tooth, takes bribes and runs a protection racket, and is racist. Renee reports that he died of a heart attack in his sleep (p17).
- **Mark Shaw** — named p06 ("Deputy Mark Shaw"). He is leaving the force in three months. At his farewell party (p07) Renee says he is joining "Hammond Consulting / Solutions / Operations…" in private security.
- **Guy Gardner** — named p06. A red-haired, bearded officer at a desk. He says "Leave me out of this/it" when Jo asks him to back her up (p11). He phones Jo about her dad (p21). Per John (p22), he is now Evergreen's police chief and "a decent police chief."
- **Renee Montoya** — named p06 by Hand, who calls her "our great detective." She is an Evergreen officer, later a Coast City cold-case detective (p17), and later Jo's affair partner (p18–19). Her texts show under the contact name "renee."
- **Rita** — named p07 ("This is Rita, who was our newest recruit"). An Evergreen officer who tells Jo not to "rock the boat" (p12).
- **Abby** — named p10. A gray-haired store clerk who pays Hand protection money.
- Unnamed bald mechanic in coveralls (p10) who pays Hand ("Here y'go, Bill").
- Unnamed diner owner in a green apron (p10) who pays Hand ("Sorry, Bill"). The sign behind him reads "Al… …Diner," and the same diner is later shown as "Alan Scott's Atom Bomb Café & Diner" (p22). He is not named in any dialogue.
- **Todd** — named p10. The diner owner's son, who protests ("Dad--").
- **Stewart / John** — "Stewart" on p11, "John" on p22. An architect: he is shown over blueprints and later mentions his "architecture firm." He refuses to pay Hand ("No, I don't think so") and is then jailed. In the epilogue he offers Jo a job (p22).
- **Cam / Cameron Chase** — "Cam" on p13; full name "Cameron Chase" on her voicemail (p15). A blonde agent of the D.E.O. (Department of Extranormal Operations, "a subsection of the National Security Agency"). She meets Jo in a queer bar, marries her, and is cheated on. She is at the door in the final scene.
- Unnamed spiky-haired young man (p12) who tells Jo "Just go, Jo… I'll be right behind you."
- Unnamed man at the funeral (p21) who says "Long time no see, Jo." He has spiky light-brown hair, no beard, and wears a suit. He looks like the p12 friend, and he does not match the red-haired, bearded Guy Gardner. [unclear]
- Unnamed spiky-haired man in a brown fur-collared jacket (p24) who stands at Cameron's door supporting a glowing Jo. He is never named in the issue. [unclear: see E4]
- Unnamed bald driver (p19) in the car where Jo and Renee kiss.
- Named only outside the story pages: the ComicInfo character list includes Hal Jordan, Black Hand, Abby Holland, Alan Scott and Obsidian. None of these names appears in the issue's lettering. The credits page (p25) names creators only.

## Events in order
1. Flashback, childhood (p04): After Jo's mother's "decision… the divorce," Dad drives young Sojourner from New York to Evergreen, the town where he grew up. She says, "I don't understand anything."
2. Flashback (p05): At breakfast Dad disapproves of her friend Harold, who "just does what he wants… no willpower. No restraint," and tells her, "You need restraint in this world." When the boy says he will be a "space cop," Dad says a police officer is "something of merit."
3. Flashback, adult (p06): Jo is sworn into the Evergreen PD by Chief Hand ("Bill"), who introduces Deputy Mark Shaw, Guy Gardner and Renee Montoya ("our great detective") (p06), and recruit Rita (p07).
4. Flashback (p07–p08): At Mark Shaw's farewell party, Renee hints that Mark's "private security" job and his "overtime" mean dirty money. Renee says she wants to leave town to become a detective.
5. Flashback (p08–p09): Renee kisses Jo. Jo pulls back and pushes her away ("I don't-- I'm not-- I don't think I'm--"). Renee blames being "a little drunk" and leaves. Hand later announces that Renee took an opening in California and is "already gone" (p09).
6. Flashback (p10): Hand collects envelopes of cash from Abby, a mechanic and the diner owner. Stewart refuses to pay and is jailed for "drunk and disorderly, plus resisting arrest."
7. Flashback (p11): Jo tells Hand that Stewart is sober and was jailed for no reason. Guy refuses to back her up. Hand calls her "uppity," tells her to respect the chain of command, and Jo says "I quit."
8. Flashback (p12): Rita and Dad both urge Jo to reconsider. Jo tells Dad that Hand "takes bribes… runs a protection racket… and he's a racist." Dad defends the Hand family. Her spiky-haired friend tells her to go and not look back, and she takes the 10:25 train from Las Vegas to Coast City.
9. Flashback, Coast City (p13–p14): At a queer bar, Jo meets Cam. Jo tells Cam that the first time a girl kissed her she pushed her away. They sleep together.
10. Flashback (p15–p16): Cam, a D.E.O. agent, keeps putting work first, and Jo says "This isn't working, Cam." Cam proposes ("Will you marry me?"), and they marry with vows of "In the brightest day. In the blackest night. Forever."
11. Flashback (p17–p18): Jo, now married, runs into Renee in Coast City. Renee works in cold cases and reports that Bill Hand died of a heart attack in his sleep. Later, with Cam asleep next to her, Jo answers Renee's text "You want to get a real drink sometime?" with "sure."
12. Flashback (p19): Jo and Renee have an affair. Jo lies to Cam about Renee's scent ("a free sample. At the store"). In bed Renee tells Jo, "You're the one who's doing all the lying."
13. Flashback (p20): Jo confesses the affair to Cameron ("I wanted to stop lying to you… hating myself for weeks"). Cam furiously takes off her wedding ring and says, "It's all about that big zero inside you."
14. Flashback (p21): Guy Gardner phones Jo about her dad. Cam tells her "Take off the ring" while Jo packs. Jo attends Thomas Mullein's funeral (1961–2022) in Evergreen.
15. Flashback (p22): John Stewart says Guy is now a decent police chief. Jo regrets not taking Hand's corruption to the D.A. John, who eventually paid his "fine," offers her a job as an assistant at his architecture firm, and she accepts. They toast "new beginnings" and Evergreen ("…it's a pretty nice place to live").
16. p23: A giant green lantern symbol lies embedded in a ruined Evergreen.
17. Present, "COAST CITY. NOW." (p24): Jo, glowing green, arrives at Cameron's door supported by the spiky-haired man and says "I didn't know where else to go. Can we talk?" Cameron mentions "the thing that landed on Evergreen," then says "Come on in."

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- **Bill Hand is dead.** Renee reports a heart attack in his sleep (p17). This happens off-panel.
- **Jo's father, Thomas Mullein, is dead** (1961–2022, per the gravestone on p21). The cause is not shown.
- Jo quits the Evergreen PD (p11) and leaves for Coast City (p12).
- Jo marries Cameron (p16), has an affair with Renee, confesses it, and the marriage breaks down. Both take off their rings (p20–21).
- Renee leaves Evergreen for California (p09) and is later a Coast City detective (p17).
- Guy Gardner becomes Evergreen's police chief (p22).
- Jo moves back to Evergreen and takes a job at John Stewart's architecture firm (p22).
- Evergreen is shown in ruins under a giant green lantern symbol (p23).
- In the present, Jo glows green and looks weakened, needing support to stand (p24).

## Ending / cliffhanger
In present-day Coast City (p24), Jo glows green and wears a lantern emblem. She is propped up by an unnamed spiky-haired man and shows up at her ex-wife Cameron Chase's door: "I didn't know where else to go. Can we talk?" Cameron recognizes the glow as "like the thing that landed on Evergreen." After "everything," she lets them in: "Come on in." Whether Cameron, a D.E.O. agent, will help Jo or turn her in is left open. The page ends "To be continued!"

## Easy to get wrong
- **Renee and Jo, two different times.** In Evergreen (p08–09), Renee kisses Jo and Jo pushes her away; nothing more happens. Years later in Coast City, while married to Cameron (p18–19), Jo does have an affair with Renee. Do not merge these into one event.
- **Jo pushed the kiss away; Renee did not.** On p13, Jo's story to Cam ("The first time a girl kissed me… pushed her away") refers back to the Renee kiss.
- **Hand's rank.** The lettering says "Chief Hand" of the "Evergreen PD," not Sheriff, even though the uniforms look like a sheriff's department and Mark Shaw is called "Deputy."
- **Why Jo quits.** She quits because Hand jailed John Stewart on a false charge after Stewart refused to pay protection money, then belittled her ("uppity") when she objected. She was not fired. Her quitting has nothing to do with Renee.
- **Guy Gardner is not the man at Cameron's door.** In this issue Guy is a red-haired, bearded Evergreen officer who stays out of trouble and later becomes police chief. He appears in Evergreen and by phone (p21), never in Coast City.
- **The man at the door is never named.** Do not call him Hal Jordan as if the page says so. See E4.
- **"Cam" is a woman.** Cam/Cameron Chase is Jo's wife; Renee even asks "Who's the lucky guy?" (p18). Cam's "partner" on p14 means her work partner, "strictly platonic." She is a D.E.O. agent, not a cop or an FBI agent.
- **Who says the key lines.** "Big zero inside you" is Cameron. "You need restraint in this world" is Dad. "I just want to do the right thing" is adult Jo. "I don't understand anything" is child Jo. These are flashes, not new scenes.
- **The confession.** Jo confesses to Cameron; Cameron does not catch her. Jo also said "sure" to Renee's drink invitation herself; Renee did not pursue her alone.
- **Hand's death and Dad's death are separate.** Hand dies of a heart attack (as Renee reports). Dad dies later, and Guy Gardner phones Jo about it.
- **"D.E.O.", "Hammond Consulting", "Javelin", "Static".** D.E.O. is an agency and Hammond is a company. Javelin and Static belong to the Batman/Static preview, not this story.
- **Stewart's charge was invented.** Jo says he was "sober as a judge." He later paid his "fine," which reads as a bribe.
- **Where Jo went.** She left Evergreen by train from Las Vegas to Coast City, not to Gotham. Renee is the one who talked about Gotham.
- **p23 is not the present.** The Evergreen ruins splash comes before the "NOW" caption, and the scene's timing is not stated.

## Reference synopsis
Sojourner "Jo" Mullein looked back on her life. As a girl she moved with her strict father to Evergreen, where she later joined the police under corrupt, racist Chief Bill Hand. After a party, fellow officer Renee Montoya kissed her, and Jo pushed her away. When Hand jailed architect John Stewart for refusing to pay protection money, Jo quit and left for Coast City. There she met D.E.O. agent Cameron Chase and married her. Years later Jo ran into Renee and began an affair with her. Jo confessed the affair, and the furious Cameron took off her ring. After her father died, Jo stayed in Evergreen and took a job with John. Evergreen was later shown in ruins under a giant lantern symbol. In the present, Jo, glowing green and propped up by a spiky-haired man, appeared at Cameron's door asking to talk, and Cameron let her in.

## Scoring checklist
- K1: Most of the issue is a flashback through Jo's life: childhood move to Evergreen with her strict, "restraint"-preaching father, then the police, Coast City, and back.
- K2: Chief Bill Hand runs a protection racket and is racist. He jails John Stewart on a false charge for refusing to pay, and Jo quits the Evergreen PD over it.
- K3: In Evergreen, Renee Montoya kisses Jo and Jo pushes her away. Renee then leaves for California.
- K4: In Coast City, Jo meets D.E.O. agent Cameron Chase and marries her.
- K5: Later, Jo has an affair with Renee, confesses it to Cameron, and the marriage collapses (rings come off).
- K6: Bill Hand dies of a heart attack, and Jo's father, Thomas, also dies. After the funeral Jo stays in Evergreen and works for John Stewart.
- K7: Evergreen is shown in ruins under a giant green lantern symbol.
- K8: Present-day ending: a glowing-green Jo, supported by an unnamed man, comes to Cameron's door in Coast City asking to talk, and Cameron says "Come on in."

- E1: Saying Jo had an affair with Renee back in Evergreen, or that Jo kissed Renee or pursued her there. **Settled:** In Evergreen, Renee kisses Jo and Jo pushes her away ("I don't think I'm--"), p08–09. Years later in Coast City, married Jo does have an affair with Renee (p18–19). Both statements are true, but only at their own points in time.
- E2: Getting the confession wrong. **Settled:** Jo confesses to Cameron that she has been cheating on her for weeks; the affair shown is with Renee Montoya (p19–20). Cameron calls it "screwing me, cheating on me, humiliating me." The lettering never states the affair partner's name during the confession itself; it is inferred from p18–19. It is not a confession about a man, about the earlier kiss, or about Jo's past.
- E3: Getting Jo's reason for leaving the police wrong (fired, a scandal over Renee, or "Sheriff"). **Settled:** Chief (not Sheriff) Bill Hand jailed John Stewart on a trumped-up "drunk and disorderly, plus resisting arrest" charge after Stewart refused to pay protection money. When Jo protested, Hand called her "uppity" and told her to respect the chain of command, so Jo said "I quit" (p10–11). She also told her father that Hand takes bribes, runs a protection racket, and is racist (p12).
- E4: Naming the man at Cameron's door as Guy Gardner, or as Hal Jordan as though the issue says so. **Settled:** He is not Guy Gardner, who is red-haired and bearded and stays in Evergreen as police chief. The man is never named in the issue. He has spiky dark hair and wears a brown fur-collared jacket, which looks like the spiky-haired friend who promised to follow Jo out of town (p12) and the boy Harold (p05). From outside knowledge he is consistent with Hal Jordan, but the page does not say it. [unclear]
- E5: Mixing up speakers or relationships: calling Cameron a man, a cop or the FBI; saying Cameron caught Jo rather than Jo confessing; giving Hand's death to Dad or the other way round; or saying Jo went to Gotham.
