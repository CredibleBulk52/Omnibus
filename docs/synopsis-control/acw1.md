## Characters
- **Selina Kyle / Catwoman**: named in captions p04 ("My name is Selina Kyle. I'm Catwoman."), called "Selina!" in the p10 flashback. Narrator and protagonist. A master thief turning 25 today, born in Cuba, raised in Gotham foster care, now living in London and planning to retire.
- **Jonesy**: first named p05 ("Jonesy, initiate scan"). The AI in Selina's suit and helmet (scans, alerts, biometrics, radiographic vision). Speaks in jagged-outlined balloons.
- **the Calicos**: named p05 ("Like my old crew, the Calicos"). Selina's old teenage crew. The copycats dress like them. The group fell apart (p20).
- **Copycats**: unnamed. Women in older-style Catwoman costumes (goggles, whips, guns). Four arrive at p05 after a fifth is caught at p03–04; Selina calls them "the copycats" on p23. They demand "Tell us where it is!", then flee and blow up her penthouse with Semtex.
- **Dante Calderone**: named p08 ("Incoming call. Dante Calderone."). Red hair, eyepatch, sunglasses, military-style gear. He manages or arranges Selina's new villa in Sicily.
- **Joe**: named p10. Selina's abusive foster father in Gotham, 12 years ago. He has a gambling debt to the Falcones (p11).
- **Maureen**: mentioned only, p10. Joe bought the bracelet for her.
- **the Falcones**: mentioned only, p11. Joe owes them gambling money.
- **Unnamed woman in white**: shown only on Selina's postcard (p11, p22), standing at a cliff's edge in the Sierra Maestra.
- **Panther**: unnamed black big cat living on the Sicily estate (p13, growl "GRRRWLL"). She has mauled two of Dante's men. Selina refuses to have her removed.
- **Holly / Holly Robinson**: "Holly?" p15. Full name given by Jonesy's scan on p25. Selina's estranged childhood friend and former Calicos partner. She shows up with a birthday cake, has a black eye, and is abducted by the end. Present day she has blonde hair with a black streak; she is drawn orange-haired in the flashback and in the chase lighting.
- **Gotham police officers**: unnamed, p17. They chase young Holly, who took one officer's cuffs.
- **King Rupert**: named p18. Young Selina's calico cat. She named him when she was ten.
- **Waylon**: mentioned only, p31 ("Waylon taught me street fighting. UFC moves, boxing."). Never shown.
- **The "mystery woman" / "leader" / Cassandra Cain**: appears from p25 as a helmeted rider with a sword (Jonesy counts her among the seven assailants). Selina calls her "the leader" and "mystery woman" (p25). She is unmasked and named only on p33 ("We are very proud of you, Cassandra Cain").
- **Unnamed caller ("We")**: voice only, p33. Praises Cassandra by phone. Identity not shown.
- **Wandering Rose**: p32. Not a person. Selina asks Jonesy for "the last known coordinates of the Wandering Rose". What it is (ship, place, person) is [unclear].

## Events in order
1. PRESENT (London, night; in medias res): a costumed copycat breaks into Selina's apartment and takes a sunflower painting off the wall. Selina's caption says the thrill of the score isn't what she's about (p03).
2. PRESENT: Selina, in her high-tech suit, brings the copycat down with tranquilizer darts ("BUDDA BUDDA") and introduces herself in captions as Selina Kyle, Catwoman (p04). Jonesy finds no fingerprints and no ID on her (p05).
3. PRESENT: Jonesy warns of more intruders. Four more armed women dressed like her old crew, the Calicos, open fire and demand "Tell us where it is!" (p05).
4. PRESENT: Selina fights them in her apartment. She says the only thing she cares about there is an old postcard they'll never find, and lists her gear: Kevlar suit, a prehensile conductive tail, and an EMP generator in the helmet's left ear (p06–07 spread). The copycats run, and she hears beeping and smells Semtex (p06–07).
5. PRESENT: The bomb explodes ("BOOM!") and blows Selina out of the building. Caption: "Happy #$%&*# birthday to me" (p07).
6. FLASHBACK, "Earlier that morning": Selina wakes up. Dante Calderone calls ("How does 25 feel, Miss Kyle?") to say her villa is well ahead of schedule, and she says she'll come today (p08). She visits her "Cat Cave," shuts it down, and says she is retiring at 25. The "Wildcat" there sits under a sheet, never used (p09).
7. FLASHBACK, Gotham, "12 years ago to the day": young Selina escapes through a window from her foster father Joe, who catches her with a bracelet he bought for Maureen. She tells him she knows he uses her foster-care checks to pay gambling debts to the Falcones, then runs across rooftops (p10–11). Captions: she came from Cuba as a baby, and her one keepsake is a postcard of a woman in white on a Sierra Maestra cliff (p11).
8. SAME DAY, "Madonie, Sicily. Now.": Dante gives Selina a tour of her new clifftop villa. She turns down support staff, and they agree the place is "forgotten" once he leaves. She refuses to have the panther that mauled two of his men removed: "She just wants to be left alone" (p12–13).
9. SAME DAY, "Back in London" that night: Selina toasts "Cheers to 25" alone. She hears a creak, grabs a knife, and throws it, hitting a birthday cake. The cake-bearer is Holly, whom she hasn't seen in seven years (p14–16). Holly got past Selina's security, and Selina finds a black eye under her hair (p16).
10. FLASHBACK, Gotham, "10 years ago": Selina hides runaway Holly from cops. Holly had smuggled a 3D-printed screwdriver to her mother in lockup and stolen an officer's cuffs. They meet over Selina's cat King Rupert, and young Holly also has a black eye (p17–18).
11. PRESENT: Holly asks for help with a "job" she can't do alone. Selina refuses ("I only work alone now"). They argue about the Calicos' breakup ("you just left" / "I cared a little too much and I paid the price"). Holly leaves with "You know I always land on my feet" (p19–20).
12. PRESENT: Selina cuts the cake and finds a small blue sphere hidden inside ("What the hell is this?"). Her captions explain the postcard's back says "Nuestro corazón" and is signed by ten tiny names, which she later scratched out (p21–22).
13. PRESENT: The alarm fails to go off. Costumed intruders enter, and Selina suits up ("Thanks a lot, Holly"). This catches up with the opening scene (p22).
14. PRESENT (after the explosion): Falling with damaged gear, Selina has Jonesy deploy a carbon-fiber net. She survives with a dislocated shoulder and a broken ankle while the copycats ride away on motorcycles (p23).
15. PRESENT: With the penthouse and her fresh-start plans gone, she uses the Wildcat motorcycle for the first time (p23–24). Jonesy detects seven assailants. Number seven is Holly Robinson, held captive by the "leader," who also has the sphere (p24–25).
16. PRESENT: The leader causes a pileup. Selina launches "Apex Mode," which turns the Wildcat into an armored, cat-like form (p25–26), and catches up ("Give me my friend back") (p27–28).
17. PRESENT: The leader matches her blow for blow with swords, without a sound. Selina's training from Waylon and her own studies can't win it (p29–31). She "shifts focus" and lashes the leader with her whip (p32).
18. PRESENT: The leader escapes with Holly, but Selina now holds the blue sphere. She asks Jonesy for the last known coordinates of the "Wandering Rose" and says "I'm gonna need more firepower" (p32).
19. PRESENT, "Elsewhere…": In a warehouse, the leader ties up Holly, who is alive and quipping. A call comes in: "You did an excellent job tonight. We are very proud of you, Cassandra Cain." The unmasked leader is shown. To be continued (p33).

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- No on-panel deaths. The copycats escape the blast on motorcycles (p23). The tranquilized copycat's fate after the explosion is not shown.
- Selina: dislocated shoulder and broken ankle (Jonesy scan, p23). Her suit gear is damaged in the explosion.
- Selina's London penthouse is destroyed by Semtex. With it go her plans for a "fresh start" and her retirement at 25 (p07, p23).
- Holly arrives with a black eye (p16) and is abducted (p25), held tied up at the end (p33).
- Two of Dante's men were mauled by the panther before the issue starts (reported, p13).
- Selina had shut down the Cat Cave and bought a Sicilian villa to retire to (p09, p12). By the end she is back in action and uses the Wildcat for the first time.
- Selina now has the mysterious blue sphere (p32).

## Ending / cliffhanger
Selina gets the blue sphere back but fails to rescue Holly, who is taken away by the silent, sword-wielding "leader." Selina sets out after the "Wandering Rose" for more firepower. In the last scene the leader, revealed as Cassandra Cain, is praised by phone by an unseen "we" for an "excellent job tonight." Holly sits tied up in a warehouse. To be continued.

## Easy to get wrong
- **Two different "Catwomen."** The woman stealing the painting on p03 is a copycat intruder. Selina is the one who tranquilizes her on p04. The "Hey there" at the end of p03 is Selina speaking to the intruder.
- **Non-linear chronology.** The opening (p03–07) happens that night. "Earlier that morning" (p08) starts a long flashback through the same day, and p23 returns to the explosion. **"Madonie, Sicily. Now."** (p12) means the same day, after the Gotham flashback. It is not the issue's real present, and the Sicily trip comes before the London attack.
- **Two different flashbacks:** "12 years ago to the day" (Selina at 13, foster father Joe, p10–11) and "10 years ago" (meeting Holly, p17–18).
- **Cassandra Cain is the sword-wielding abductor/"leader," working for an unseen "we."** She is not shown as Batgirl or an ally, and she is named only on the last page. Her name is not "Mystery Woman."
- **Holly Robinson is not a villain or the bomber here.** She is Selina's old friend and Calicos partner who brings a cake with the sphere hidden inside and is then kidnapped. Selina's "Thanks a lot, Holly" means Holly brought trouble to her door. It does not mean Holly attacked her.
- **Jargon, not characters:** "Jonesy" = suit AI. "the Wildcat" = Selina's vehicle (motorcycle). "Apex Mode" = its transformed combat mode. "the Calicos" = Selina's old crew (and "calico" is also the kind of cat King Rupert is). "Cat Cave" = her gear vault. "Wandering Rose" = unexplained target, not a person as far as shown.
- **Copycat count:** five in the apartment (p24). Jonesy counts seven on the road, which includes Holly as "number seven." The leader was a separate figure "in the shadows."
- **What the copycats want:** they demand "Tell us where it is!" Selina thinks they mean her prized postcard, but the plot points to the sphere. The postcard is never stolen.
- **Selina is Cuban-born** (arrived as a baby across 93 miles of sea) and raised in Gotham foster care/orphanages. She is **25** and lives in **London**, not Gotham. The panther belongs to the Sicily estate and is not Selina's pet. The cat **King Rupert is Selina's cat**, not Holly's.
- **Dante Calderone** handles Selina's villa. He has no on-page tie to mobsters. The Falcones are mentioned only as Joe's creditors.
- **Selina retiring is the setup, not the outcome.** By the end she is back in costume, injured, chasing Holly.
- **No one dies.** Don't claim the copycats or Holly were killed.
- **Mainstream-DC assumptions:** Waylon (outside knowledge: mainstream Waylon Jones is Killer Croc) appears only as the name of Selina's street-fighting teacher. Nothing on the page ties him to Croc, and "Jonesy" being linked to him is speculation only. Don't assume Selina is linked to Batman or Gotham's heroes. No Batman appears.

## Reference synopsis
On her 25th birthday, master thief Selina Kyle had shut down her "Cat Cave" in London and toured her new retirement villa in Sicily, arranged by Dante Calderone. That night, her estranged childhood friend Holly Robinson, a former member of their old crew the Calicos, broke in with a birthday cake and asked for help with a job. Selina refused, and Holly left. Inside the cake Selina found a mysterious blue sphere. Women dressed as Calicos then attacked, demanding "it," and blew up her penthouse with Semtex. Selina survived the fall with a dislocated shoulder and broken ankle, took out her never-used Wildcat motorcycle, and chased a silent, sword-wielding leader who had kidnapped Holly. She recovered the sphere but lost Holly. Flashbacks showed Selina's Cuban origins, abusive foster father, and meeting Holly. Selina sought the "Wandering Rose" for firepower, while the leader, revealed as Cassandra Cain, was praised by unseen superiors.
