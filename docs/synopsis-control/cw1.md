## Events in order
1. The New Warriors film an episode of their reality show from a stakeout in Stamford, Connecticut, while a WTNH-Channel 8 control room watches. Speedball counts four escaped villains: Cobalt Man, Coldheart, Speedfreek and Nitro (p02).
2. Microbe says they are out of their league. Speedball overrules him for "the ratings", says this episode could make up for Nova leaving the show, and mentions six months of finding nothing in the Midwest (p03).
3. Coldheart spots them ("We've been marked"). The villains shout "It's a raid!" and the Warriors attack (p03–p04).
4. Speedball beats Speedfreek on camera (p05). Namorita and Night Thrasher fight Coldheart, and Microbe's bacteria rust Cobalt Man (p06).
5. Namorita chases Nitro and slams him into a school bus in front of an elementary school full of children (p07).
6. Nitro says "You're playing with the big boys now" and explodes. The blast engulfs the children and levels the neighborhood (p08).
7. Title splash: Iron Man and Captain America stand in the ruins while heroes and firefighters search the rubble (p09).
8. Iron Man says Nitro reportedly escaped in a pickup truck. Cap cites the FEMA chief's estimate of "eight or nine hundred casualties", and Iron Man says Speedball knew they were out of their league (p10).
9. The X-Men help. Marvel Girl (Rachel) lifts rubble to free trapped children, and Wolverine resents the Sentinels shadowing the X-Men (p10–p12). Goliath predicts witch hunts, and Ms Marvel says maybe the public is right (p12).
10. On CNN, She-Hulk says banning heroes is impossible but training them and making them carry badges is reasonable (p13).
11. At the Stamford memorial service, a grieving mother spits on Tony Stark. She blames him for funding the Avengers and super-hero vigilantism and says Damien's blood is on his hands. Peter Parker photographs the scene (p13–p15).
12. A CNN pundit calls for super-heroes to be tested and registered (p15).
13. Outside the Lazer Club, a crowd calls Johnny Storm a "baby-killer". Someone smashes a bottle over his head and the crowd beats him. A news anchor calls it the latest in a series of attacks on New York's super-community and reports pressure on the President for reform (p16–p17).
14. At the Baxter Building, heroes debate the coming government proposal. Iron Man backs becoming accountable public employees, and Falcon defends masks. The Thing mentions that Johnny ended up in the hospital last night. Spider-Man worries about his wife and aunt (p18–p20).
15. On the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier, Maria Hill tells Cap the proposal goes to a vote in two weeks and could be law within a month. She wants Cap to lead the Avengers in hunting unregistered heroes with a new anti-superhuman response unit, and Cap refuses (p21–p23).
16. Hill orders tranquilizers. Cap fights through the armored S.H.I.E.L.D. troopers, smashes out of the Helicarrier, jumps onto an incoming fighter jet (War-Hawk One) and flies off on it (p24–p30).
17. A bloodied Hill says, "You idiot. We were trying to save lives." At the Baxter Building, the Watcher appears, which Doctor Strange says "does not bode well" (p31).
18. In Washington, "Justice for Damien" protesters gather at the White House, and a TV report credits Miriam Sharpe with Congress's swift response. Officials hear that Cap landed the jet in a football field and took the pilot for a hamburger and fries (p32).
19. With Cap underground as a figurehead for dissent, Iron Man, flanked by Reed Richards and a hero who looks like Yellowjacket, says: "You push ahead with registration as planned, gentlemen. Leave Captain America to us." (p33)

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- The Stamford explosion (p08) kills the schoolchildren and many others. The FEMA chief's estimate, quoted by Cap, is "eight or nine hundred casualties" (p10). Damien Sharpe is one of the dead children (p15, p32).
- The New Warriors are presumed killed. Speedball is called "the dead" by the pundit (p15). Namorita, Night Thrasher and Microbe are not seen after the blast, and none of their deaths is shown on panel.
- Nitro survived and was "reportedly" seen fleeing in a pickup truck (p10). He is still at large.
- Johnny Storm is bottled and beaten by a mob (p17) and is hospitalized (p19).
- Maria Hill is bloodied (p31), and several S.H.I.E.L.D. troopers are injured in Cap's escape (p25–p28).
- Captain America becomes a fugitive and goes underground (p30, p33).
- Iron Man sides with the government and offers to bring Cap in (p33).
- The registration proposal is pending: a vote in two weeks, possibly law within a month (p23). It is not law by the end of the issue.

## Ending / cliffhanger
Cap has escaped S.H.I.E.L.D. and gone underground. The Watcher has appeared at the Baxter Building, a sign of great upheaval. At the White House, Iron Man, with Reed Richards and the Yellowjacket-looking hero beside him, tells officials to push ahead with registration and "Leave Captain America to us" (p33), setting up a hero-versus-hero manhunt.

## Easy to get wrong
- The Registration Act is not passed in this issue. It is a proposal headed for a vote in two weeks (p23).
- Cap does not fight Iron Man here. He fights S.H.I.E.L.D. troopers under Maria Hill and escapes on a fighter jet. Cap and Iron Man only talk, in the Stamford ruins (p10).
- Spider-Man does not unmask. That happens in #2. Here he only talks about protecting his wife and aunt (p20).
- Goliath does not die. He appears alive (p09, p12). His death by the clone Thor is in #4.
- There is no clone Thor, no Negative Zone prison, no Punisher, no Secret Avengers base, and no Sue Storm defection. All of those come later.
- Cap is not arrested and does not surrender. That is #7. He escapes.
- Nitro is not captured. He is still at large after a reported pickup-truck escape (p10). His capture by Wolverine comes in a tie-in, not this issue.
- Speedball's survival and his later identity as Penance are not shown. This issue only implies the New Warriors died.
- The casualty figure is "eight or nine hundred casualties" (a FEMA estimate, p10). Do not substitute "600" or another number from outside sources.
- The angry mother at the funeral is not named on those pages (p14–p15). The name Miriam Sharpe appears only on the TV caption on p32, and her son is Damien.
- The villain who explodes is Nitro, not Speedfreek or Coldheart. Namorita, not Speedball, was fighting him at the bus.
- The Johnny Storm attack is by civilians outside a nightclub, not by villains or S.H.I.E.L.D.
- The cover blurb (p01) says the act requires heroes to "unmask themselves before the government". That is cover copy, not a story event.
- In the lettering, "Yellowjacket" is never named, Hank Pym is never named, Daredevil is never named and Sue Storm is never named. Their presence is inferred from the art.

## Reference synopsis
The New Warriors filmed their reality show while raiding four escaped villains in Stamford, Connecticut. While Namorita fought him beside a school, Nitro exploded, killing hundreds, many of them children; the FEMA chief estimated eight or nine hundred casualties. In the aftermath, a grieving mother spat on Tony Stark at the memorial and blamed him for her son Damien's death. A mob beat Johnny Storm outside a club, and public pressure built for super-hero registration. At the Baxter Building the heroes split over the coming proposal, and Iron Man backed accountability. On the S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier, Maria Hill asked Captain America to lead the hunt for unregistered heroes. He refused, fought off her troops and escaped on a fighter jet. At the White House, Iron Man told officials to push registration ahead and leave Captain America to him. The act itself had not yet passed.
