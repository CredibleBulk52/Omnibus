- **Mr. Hammond / Hector Hammond** (p04 "Mr. Hammond", first shown p05; full name "Hector Hammond" said by Kari p22) — boss in a red jacket drinking New York Sours, running the ooze experiment.
- **"Doc"** (unnamed; p04–p05, p23; called "Doc" by Hammond p23) — bearded scientist in a yellow hazmat suit and glasses, runs the tanks and readings.
- Unnamed hazmat worker (p03–p04) — locks the hose onto Todd's tank ("Mr. Hammond says" Todd isn't useful anymore).
- Unnamed woman in pink suit with tablet (p05) — Hammond's aide; never named.
- **Jo / Mullein / Jo Mullein** (p07 "Jo"; p09 "Mullein") — the protagonist; shot twice, kills Goldface, has green power.

- **Agent Baz / Simon Baz** (p17 "Agent Baz", p18 "Simon"/"Simon Baz") — agent in a black suit and green luchador-style mask; Cam's best friend; was at Jo's wedding and divorce.

- **Cam / Cameron / Chase** (p13 "Chase", p16 "Cam", p19 "Cameron"; on-panel from p19) — blonde, ponytailed, smoking agent; the apartment is hers; strongly implied to be Jo's ex-spouse.
- Flashback figures (p12): a child with afro puffs (by implication young Jo) and a blond boy who stepped on a nail (unnamed); a greyscale blonde woman shouting "you deserve this... eat it, Jo Mullein". She is not named, but her look matches Cam.
- Named only in metadata/credits, not in the story lettering: Carol Ferris, "Sojourner" Mullein (Jo's full name), "Obsidian" as a character (the word appears only as Hammond's/Doc's label for the result, p23), "Kari Limbo" (surname), "Hal". Carol Ferris doesn't appear named anywhere.

## Events in order
1. (Present, Hammond's hangar) A voice tells Todd Rice he is "an unknown within the spectrum of light," that "Qard has found you" and "you must be without fear"; a hazmat worker above the hatch heard a voice too (p03–p04).
2. Todd, injured and begging for painkillers, is told he's "not so useful anymore, Mr. Hammond says"; a hose is locked onto his tank (p04).
3. The Doc briefs Hammond: tank one holds about 200 gallons of black ooze from the Evergreen site, tank two holds Rice; question is what happens to a man submerged in the substance "that turned Jordan into what he became." Hammond says "hit it" (p05).
4. (Present, Cam's apartment) Goldface stands over Jo, who has been shot twice and is "dying on your ex's floor"; Jordan is unconscious on the couch. Gold bullets, suit and face: "the one thing your green whammy can't touch" (p06).
5. Goldface offers to spare her if she says "where the Green Lantern went"; as she mumbles, she flings a glob of her own blood into his face (p07).
6. He screams and thinks he's hurt ("There's blood!"), and she kicks him off. Then he realises "Not my blood" and calls the deal off (p08–p09).
7. Jo crawls into the kitchen, slams the door and fuses the handle/lock with green energy (p09–p10).
8. While he kicks at the door, Jo empties the junk drawer ("can't heal unless..."), finds pliers and digs the two gold bullets out of her own body ("One. Two--", "That's one... and one to go") (p10–p13).
9. (Flashback, during the bullet removal) A childhood memory of a "most painful thing" talk by a sprinkler ("To me, or someone else?"), and a greyscale memory of a blonde woman telling Jo "you deserve this... eat every bite" (p11 caption to p12).
10. Goldface breaks in, threatening "Calling Chase? Fine! I'll kill her too!" as the second bullet drops (p13).
11. Jo, healed and floating with Medusa-like green hair, says "Know what isn't gold? These" and drives the pliers into his face at the eye ("Dig the gold out of that"); he falls dead (p14–p15).
12. Simon Baz and Kari arrive, find the blood and Jordan in a coma, and look for Cam. Jo says "Ask your friend in the kitchen"; Simon draws his gun ("Cam?") and Jo blasts him through a wall (p16–p18).
13. Jo recognises Simon Baz from her wedding; he says he was at her divorce too and accuses her of crushing his best friend's heart, then demands to know where Cameron is (p18–p19).
14. Cam walks in unharmed, smoking: "I'm right here, Simon" ... "So what happened to my apartment?" (p19).
15. Jo explains she let Goldface in because he looked and sounded like John Stewart ("magic face-change gas"). Cam asks how he knew about gold bullets and where to find her, and "Who sold you out?" Cam recaps that the "Green Lantern" that hit Evergreen was flown by Jo's green power and crashed by "her gold ring"; "the green doesn't like gold" (p20–p21).
16. Kari uses psychometry on the corpse: he is Keith Kenyon, who worked for Hector Hammond. Cam: "We should go. Now." (p21–p22)
17. (Present, hangar) Monitors read zero from Rice (no ECG, no brain waves) while data still comes from the ooze; the Doc says they've made "an Obsidian shell with no humanity at all inside it." Hammond is pleased: "this is exactly what I needed" (p23).
18. A huge black crystalline creature bursts out of the tank in the hangar. TO BE CONTINUED (p24).

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- Goldface / Keith Kenyon: killed by Jo with pliers driven into his face (eye area). Kari calls him a "dead body" (p21).
- Jo: shot twice with gold bullets before the issue opens. She removes both with pliers ("a gold bullet out of my gut") and heals. She then shows a new power look (floating, snake-like green hair, glowing eyes).
- Simon Baz: thrown through a wall by Jo's green blast. He is shaken but conscious and talking.
- Jordan: in a coma on the couch (says Jo); not harmed further on-panel.
- Cam Chase: NOT shot, NOT hurt. Goldface only threatened to kill her.
- Todd Rice: submerged in black ooze. No vital signs or brain waves read from him afterward, and the result is a giant black "Obsidian" shell/creature. He isn't explicitly declared dead, and it isn't clear whether he is inside it [unclear].
- Cam's apartment wrecked (door kicked in, wall blasted, blood everywhere).

## Ending / cliffhanger
Kari's reading ties Goldface to Hector Hammond, and Cam says they must leave at once. Meanwhile in Hammond's hangar the ooze experiment has turned Todd Rice into a giant black crystalline "Obsidian" creature with no human readings. Hammond calls it "exactly what I needed." Open threads: who sold Jo out (who told Goldface about gold and where she was), where the "Green Lantern" went, Jordan's coma, and the "Qard" voice.

## Easy to get wrong
- How Goldface dies: pliers, propelled by Jo's power, into his face/eye (p14–p15). It isn't a ring, a construct or a gun. The pliers are the same ones she used on her own wounds. Jo's "gold ring" (p21) is in Cam's recap of the earlier crash, not this fight.
- Who got shot: only Jo, twice, before p06. Cam is never shot. She's the one Goldface threatens ("I'll kill her too") and she arrives later unhurt.
- "Your ex's floor" = Cam's apartment (p06, p19). The ex is Cam, not Jordan. Simon's "best friend" whose heart Jo crushed is Cam.
- "Ask your friend in the kitchen" refers to Goldface's corpse. Simon fears it's Cam, which is why he pulls a gun. Cam is not the body.
- Goldface entered disguised as John Stewart. John Stewart doesn't appear and isn't the villain.
- "Green Lantern" in Goldface's question and Cam's recap is a thing/vehicle that hit Evergreen ("called the Green Lantern by its pilot"), not Jo's title.
- Jordan is on the couch in a coma. He never wakes and does nothing. (Art inconsistency: his hair looks dark on p06 and blond on p16.)
- The blood Goldface panics about is Jo's, flung into his face (p07–p09). He wasn't wounded then.
- The p12 scenes are flashbacks (sepia childhood; greyscale angry blonde). They are not present-day events.
- "Qard," "Evergreen" (a site), "Soder Cola," "New York Sour," "Luchador," "Level Two Psychic" are jargon, not character names.
- The "Doc" in the hazmat suit is not Hammond. Hammond is the man in the red jacket with the drink. The woman in pink (p05) isn't named. She is not confirmed as Carol Ferris, and Carol Ferris isn't named in the issue.
- Kari reads Goldface's body by touch (psychometry). She doesn't read Todd Rice, Jordan or anyone else, and she names "Keith Kenyon" and "Hector Hammond."
- Arrival order: Simon Baz and Kari first (p16), then Cam (p19). Carol Ferris doesn't arrive.

## Reference synopsis
In Hector Hammond's hangar, a voice told the captive Todd Rice that "Qard" had found him, before Hammond's crew flooded his tank with black ooze from the Evergreen site. In her ex Cameron Chase's apartment, Jo Mullein lay shot twice with gold bullets by Goldface, who had entered disguised as John Stewart and wanted the "Green Lantern's" whereabouts. Jo distracted him with flung blood and barricaded herself in the kitchen. She dug both bullets out with pliers, then healed and drove the non-gold pliers into his face, killing him. Simon Baz and the psychic Kari arrived, and a clash ended when Cam walked in unharmed. Kari's psychometric reading of the corpse named him Keith Kenyon, an agent of Hammond, while the question of who betrayed Jo stayed open. In the hangar, Rice registered no life signs, and a monstrous "Obsidian" shell rose that Hammond called exactly what he needed.

- K3: Jo kills Goldface by driving the (non-gold) pliers into his face/eye with her power.
- K4: Goldface got in disguised as John Stewart; the apartment belongs to Cam Chase, Jo's ex.
- K5: Simon Baz and Kari arrive; Jo blasts Simon; Cam then appears alive and unhurt.
- K6: Kari psychometrically reads Goldface's corpse: Keith Kenyon, working for Hector Hammond.
- K7: Hammond's people submerge Todd Rice in black ooze from Evergreen.
- K8: The result is a giant black "Obsidian" shell/creature with no readings from Rice, and Hammond is pleased (cliffhanger).

- E2: Saying Cam Chase is shot, wounded or killed. Correct: Cam is unharmed; only Jo was shot. Goldface only threatened Cam.
- E3: Getting the arrivals wrong (e.g., Carol Ferris arrives, or Kari reads Todd/Jordan). Correct: Simon Baz and Kari arrive, then Cam. Kari reads Goldface's dead body (Keith Kenyon, Hammond's man). Carol Ferris doesn't appear by name.
- E4: Saying Todd Rice gains powers/escapes, or is clearly confirmed dead. Correct: he is submerged in black ooze; readings show nothing from him; an "Obsidian shell with no humanity" emerges as a huge black crystalline creature, and Hammond is pleased.
