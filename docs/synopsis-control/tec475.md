## Characters
- **The Batman** (p02 caption "And the Batman prowls"; "Batman" spoken p04): the hero. Calls Silver as **"Bruce"** by phone on p05, and on p05 the caller is drawn as a caped silhouette in a phone booth.
- **Silver St. Cloud** (p03, spoken by Batman; "Silver" p05; "Ms. St. Cloud" p03/p04): Bruce Wayne's girlfriend. She privately knows Batman is Bruce and leaves town.
- **Bruce Wayne** (p04, named by Silver): Batman's identity. Silver names him as her date for the night.
- **Deadshot** (p04, mentioned only, "that Deadshot guy"): from the previous issue. He does not appear.
- **Magda** (p08, mentioned in Batman's thoughts): "became a monster" after learning his secret. A footnote cites 'Tec #471–472.
- **Hugo Strange / "Prof. Strange"** (p08 footnote; p21, p24, p26): mentioned only. Per the p21 footnote, Thorne had him killed "three issues ago" and he has "reappeared, threatening revenge". He never appears.
- **Joe Chill** (p08, mentioned): killed Batman's parents.
- **Unnamed fishermen** (p09): a capped old fisherman and others on the docks. They show Batman their Joker-faced catch.
- **Unnamed woman in a kitchen** (p10): hears the radio report about the Joker-fish.
- **Joker's henchmen** (p10): armed men, one Black and one blond among them, who hold up the Copyright Commission. One, in a green jacket, is called **"Blue-Eyes"** by the Joker on p15.
- **The Joker** (first named p09 in Batman's line "with the Joker's face?!"; on panel p11; addressed "J-Joker" p11): the villain. He wants a copyright on the Joker-fish.
- **Mr. Francis / G. Carl Francis** (p11 "Mr. Francis"; full name "G. Carl Francis" p24, p25, p27): bald, bespectacled bureaucrat at the Copyright Commission. He refuses the Joker and is murdered. Calls himself "just a paper-pusher" (p26).
- **Rupert Thorne / "Rupe" / "Boss Thorne"** (p20 "Rupert--?"; "Boss Thorne" p21 footnote): Gotham crime boss and council figure. The Joker terrifies him and he flees.
- **Marko** (p20): Thorne's associate of 45 years ("Paisan"). He reminds Thorne of the council meeting.
- **Sergeant Stark** (p24): Thorne's driver, pushed aside when Thorne drives off himself.
- **The Commissioner** (p25, "Commissioner"): lights the Bat-signal and works with Batman at Francis's home. He is never named "Gordon" in the lettering I read.
- **Police officers** (p25–p27): guard Francis's house.
- **The Penguin** (p21, mentioned as a bidder for Batman's identity). The Penguin, the Scarecrow and the Riddler are also mentioned by Francis on p26. None of them appear.
- "Julie" (editor) signs the footnotes (p03, p08, p21).

## Events in order
1. On a stormy night when the storm "refuses to break", the Batman swings toward a room he wants, thinking "to enter it may mean my finish" (p02). After watching unseen, he appears at Silver St. Cloud's window and asks "May I come in?" (p03).
2. Batman thinks Silver knows who he is because she "called to me... last night" (footnote "last ish"). He tells her he thought she had something to tell him. She says she knows nothing and had never seen "that Deadshot guy" before (p03–p04).
3. Silver says she has a date coming to pick her up, "Bruce Wayne! Perhaps you know each other?" Batman concludes he may have made a mistake, apologizes, and leaves with "Until we meet again!" (p04).
4. Alone, Silver thinks to herself that she does know: "That was the Batman!" She recognized him from "studying your jaw" and thinks "I love Bruce Wayne" (p05, mirrored scan). Batman never hears this.
5. "Bruce" phones from a booth to say he'll be a little late. Silver claims a headache and they postpone the date. She hangs up thinking "I've got to get out of here!... where he won't find me" (p05).
6. Swinging over the city, Batman broods over whether she knows. He recalls Magda and Hugo Strange learning his secret, admits "I'm in love with that girl", and remembers his secrets have been his protection since Joe Chill. He then hears "Help! Help, police!" (p08).
7. On the docks a fisherman shows Batman that their whole catch has the Joker's face, "the same for everybody else". Batman says fear is the Joker's strongest weapon and his motives "make sense to him alone" (p09).
8. Joker-fish turn up all along the Eastern Seaboard and up and down the Western one, and they are the only topic on the morning news (p10).
9. At "high noon" armed henchmen take over the Copyright Commission ("You've got a visitor!"), and the Joker walks in on Mr. Francis (p10–p11).
10. The Joker strikes Francis across the face (p14, "TAK"). He explains he is secretly dumping a chemical that gives fish his face, and he demands a copyright so he gets a cut of every fish sale in America, "a nickel per fish-sandwich". Francis says it's impossible, because nobody can copyright fish, which are a natural resource (p14).
11. The Joker warns him, "You have until midnight to change your mind... or you'll be... dead as a mackerel!" Francis protests "It's the law!" (p15).
12. Outside, the Joker sends his men back to their dumping and tells them to alert the other crews. When "Blue-Eyes" asks what he'll do, the Joker shoves him in front of an oncoming, honking car ("mind your own business!"). The henchman's fate is not shown (p15, p20).
13. At 3:30 at the Tobacconists' Club, a jumpy Rupert Thorne meets Marko. Marko tells him "the boys" want his big plan for the Batman, since declaring him off limits isn't going over well. Thorne goes to wash up first (p20).
14. In the washroom the Joker frightens Thorne, who screams. The Joker says he knows Thorne bid for Batman's identity alongside the Penguin and the Joker himself, and suspects Thorne is behind Prof. Strange's disappearance. He says Thorne lives only because he didn't learn the identity. The Joker doesn't want the secret revealed, because "the Joker deserves the Batman" (p21).
15. The Joker tells Thorne the matter is closed if Strange's knowledge died with him, then teases "what if Strange didn't die--? Not completely--?" Thorne panics, shoves aside his driver Sergeant Stark ("I'm driving myself! I'm getting away!"), and speeds off without noticing the Bat-signal (p24).
16. Batman answers the Commissioner's signal at G. Carl Francis's uptown home. The Joker interrupts TV reruns with a "fireside chat": "at precisely twelve o'clock, I will kill G. Carl Francis!" (p24–p25).
17. Batman takes charge of police preparations. At 11:55 Francis is cordoned off in his house, has eaten nothing, and drinks only water from the police lab. Batman wishes Silver would answer his calls, since "She's been gone all day", and wishes Hugo Strange would turn up (p25–p26).
18. Gas pours in through the heating ducts, and Batman claps a gas mask on Francis (p26). As the clock strikes midnight Francis screams, collapses and dies, his face pulling into a Joker grin. Others breathed the gas without masks and lived. Batman explains it is one half of a binary compound, and the Joker "must have secretly sprayed him with the other gas when he threatened him at noon" (p27).
19. On TV the Joker gloats that Francis is dead "as I vowed". He still wants a legal claim to his Joker-fish, and if he doesn't have it "by 3 A.M., the number-two bureaucrat will feel my wrath." Batman makes no reply (p27, p30).
20. About three hundred miles west, Thorne stops on a highway for Silver, whose car has broken down. They recognize each other from Bruce Wayne's party, and she gets a lift ("It sure was lucky meeting you here!"). The storm finally begins (p30).

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- **G. Carl Francis is murdered** by the Joker's binary gas at midnight, despite police protection and a gas mask. He dies with a Joker grin (p27).
- A henchman ("Blue-Eyes") is shoved into the path of a car by the Joker. Whether he was injured is not shown (p20).
- Others at Francis's house breathe the gas and are unharmed (p27).
- **Silver St. Cloud leaves Gotham.** She cancels her date, is "gone all day", and ends up 300 miles west in Thorne's car (p05, p26, p30).
- **Rupert Thorne flees Gotham in panic**, driving himself, because he fears Hugo Strange may not be fully dead (p24, p30).
- Joker-fish are now on both U.S. coasts, and the Joker's crews keep dumping the chemical (p10, p14–p15).
- Hugo Strange's death is cast in doubt only by Thorne's fear and the Joker's taunt. Strange does not appear.

## Ending / cliffhanger
The Joker announces on TV that Francis is dead and says the "number-two bureaucrat" will feel his wrath if he doesn't get a legal claim to the Joker-fish by 3 A.M. Batman makes no reply (p30). Meanwhile, 300 miles west, Rupert Thorne picks up a stranded Silver St. Cloud as the storm finally breaks. The "Next issue" box reads: the wrap-up in "Sign of the Joker!", Detective #476 (p30). The story is a cliffhanger and continues next issue.

## Easy to get wrong
- **Who knows what about Silver.** Batman leaves believing she may *not* know his identity ("Maybe I have made a mistake"). Only her private thoughts on p05 show she knows he is Bruce Wayne. She never tells him.
- **The "Bruce" phone call** on p05 is Batman/Bruce calling Silver from a phone booth, not a separate Bruce scene. Silver is the one who cancels the date, using a headache as her excuse.
- **p05 is mirrored in this scan**, so text read straight off the image comes out backwards.
- **Name: "G. Carl Francis"** (p24, p25, p27). Before that page he is only "Mr. Francis". The bundled ComicInfo metadata wrongly says "Carl G. Francis".
- **The two deadlines.** Francis's deadline is midnight. The *next* threat is against "the number-two bureaucrat" by 3 A.M., not midnight.
- **How Francis died.** He was killed by a two-part (binary) gas. Batman deduces that one part was sprayed on him at noon and the other came through the heating ducts. The gas mask did not save him, and others who breathed the gas lived. He was not shot or poisoned through food or water.
- **Who said "nobody can copyright fish".** Francis says "Joker, it--it's impossible!" The "natural resource" line follows the Joker's "What? Impossible, you say? No--". Its speaker is [unclear] (balloon tail ambiguous), and the Joker has just said "I always answer my questions myself."
- **The Joker is *protecting* Batman's identity.** He warns Thorne off and says no one else should destroy Batman. He is not trying to learn it.
- **Thorne flees out of fear, not because he was attacked.** He fears Hugo Strange's "ghost" or survival. Thorne meets Silver by apparent chance, and she flags him down. It is not shown as a kidnapping.
- **Mentioned only, never on panel:** Deadshot, Hugo Strange, Magda, Joe Chill, the Penguin, the Scarecrow and the Riddler.
- **The Commissioner is never named "Gordon"** in the lettering. That name comes from outside knowledge.
- **There is no backup story.** Pages p17, p19, p23 and p29 are DC house ads (Power Girl, Firestorm, Superman vs. Muhammad Ali, subscriptions). The letters page (p31–p32) discusses #471–472 (Robin, Alfred, Hugo Strange), not this issue.
- **Continuity.** The story continues from the previous issue (Deadshot, "last ish") and continues into #476, "Sign of the Joker!". It is not self-contained.
- **The cover line** "Hands up, Batman! I've got you covered!" (p01) does not occur in the story. Batman and the Joker never meet face to face in this issue.

## Reference synopsis
The night after the Deadshot affair, the Batman visited Silver St. Cloud, believing she had recognized him. She denied knowing anything and mentioned a date with Bruce Wayne, so he apologized and left. Privately she knew his secret and loved him, and after "Bruce" phoned, she resolved to flee. Fishermen then showed Batman catches bearing the Joker's face, soon found on both coasts. At the Copyright Commission, the Joker demanded rights to his "Joker-fish". When G. Carl Francis said fish couldn't be copyrighted, the Joker vowed to kill him at midnight. The Joker also frightened Rupert Thorne away from Batman's identity, hinting Hugo Strange might live, and Thorne fled Gotham. Despite Batman's protection and a gas mask, Francis died at midnight, grinning, from a binary gas. The Joker threatened the next bureaucrat by 3 A.M., while Thorne, 300 miles west, picked up the departing Silver.
