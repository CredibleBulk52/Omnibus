## Characters
- **Jordan / Hal** (Hal Jordan, Green Lantern): first called "Jordan" by Sinestro on p04, "Hal" by Kilowog on p18. Called "2814" by the Guardians on p20. He comes to Oa wearing several rings, kills Sinestro and Kilowog, enters the Central Battery and leaves Oa in a new costume. The name "Parallax" never appears.
- **Sinestro**: first named on p03 ("Get out of my way, Sinestro"). The Guardians freed him from the Central Battery to stop Hal. Hal kills him.
- **The Guardians** (blue-skinned, red-robed, unnamed except Ganthet): first mentioned on p08 ("The Guardians must be mad to have released you"), first shown watching on p07. They give their last strength to Ganthet and are left dead.
- **Ganthet**: a Guardian with a ponytail, first named on p22 ("Ganthet is right"). He is the sole surviving Guardian. He re-forms a ring and gives it to a young man on Earth.
- **Kilowog**: first named on p18. A pink, porcine Green Lantern who tries to stop Hal from entering the battery. Hal kills him.
- **Unnamed young man** (Kyle Rayner by outside knowledge; **never named in this issue**): first seen on p28 outside the "Heretic Club", wearing a leather jacket and a Nine Inch Nails T-shirt. He receives the ring and transforms on p30.
- **Unnamed homeless man** (red beard, striped scarf): in the alley on p29–p30. He witnesses Ganthet vanish and the young man transform.

## Events in order
1. On Oa, Hal (wearing several power rings on both hands) confronts Sinestro by the Central Battery. Sinestro mocks the irony of Hal as the "prodigal son" and himself as "Oa's last hope". Hal says "Get out of my way, Sinestro. Or I'll kill you!" (p02–p03)
2. Hal blasts Sinestro (p04). Sinestro calls the extra rings unsporting and goads Hal into an even contest. Hal pulls off and drops the extra rings ("...without any crutches"), keeping one ring. (p05)
3. They duel with ring constructs while three Guardians watch from a viewing platform (p06–p07). Sinestro says he was imprisoned "inside the battery... reduced to less than a thought" (recap, p07).
4. Sinestro says the Guardians released him, perhaps as "the lesser of two evils". He tells how the Guardians once begged him to train recruit Jordan, and how he judged Hal a "sorry fool" who would never amount to anything: "I was right!" (p08–p09)
5. Sinestro says the Guardians are giving him his freedom for stopping Hal. Hal answers: "You're not killing anybody. Ever again!" (p10)
6. Their blasts collide in a huge explosion ("CHOOM") that throws both men back (p11).
7. The fight turns hand to hand ("No more tricks, no more rings... just you and me. For the last time!"), and Hal beats Sinestro bloody (p12–p14).
8. A beaten Sinestro says "we're finished... both of us" and tells Hal to look at himself. Hal snaps Sinestro's neck ("KRACK") mid-sentence (p14). He says "Damn you" over the body and walks toward the Central Battery (p15).
9. Hal says "No one left to stop me. Power's mine for the taking", but Kilowog ambushes him with a punch (p16–p17).
10. Hal says he did not kill the Corps, only took their rings and "left them enough to survive" (recap of earlier events, p17). Kilowog says Hal wants to get into the Central Battery "again" to take all the power (footnote cites *Emerald Dawn* #5 and *Green Lantern* #6, p17). Kilowog also mentions losing his own home and loved ones (p18).
11. Kilowog pleads, "Ya killed Sinestro, but ya ain't evil like him. Not yet." Hal says "No. I can't" and incinerates Kilowog with a ring blast (p19). He kneels over the charred body: "I've crossed the line, Kilowog... and I can't go back." (p20)
12. Hal takes off his own ring and tosses it down the steps: "I don't deserve this anymore." (p20)
13. The Guardians tell Hal (as "2814") that he will be punished. Hal calls them "impotent shams" and says he only wanted his life back and the power to "make everything right". A Guardian replies: "To tamper with what has been is the most grievous crime of all." (p20–p21)
14. Hal enters the Central Battery (p22). Ganthet objects to doing nothing. The other Guardians agree they must act and choose Ganthet. They pour their energy into him and wither, while the battery cracks and erupts in green light. (p22–p23)
15. Hal emerges in a new armored costume (full-page splash, p24). He walks past the Guardians' withered bodies, crushes a discarded Green Lantern ring under his boot ("SKRAK"), and flies off into space (p25–p26).
16. Ganthet climbs out from among his dead brothers, mourns them, and gathers the crushed ring's remains. Calling himself "the sole remaining Guardian", he re-forms the ring as "a symbol... once again" and departs in a green sphere (p26–p27).
17. On Earth, outside the Heretic Club, a young man sees a green streak fall from the sky "again" (footnote: GL #48, recap/reference). It lands beside him. (p28)
18. Ganthet tells him "You shall have to do", hands him the ring ("Take it... What you must"), and vanishes (p29).
19. The young man puts on the ring. He transforms into a Green Lantern costume in front of the homeless man: "I think my life just got a lot more complicated." (p29–p30)

## Deaths, injuries, major changes
- **Sinestro dies**: Hal snaps his neck by hand after a ring duel and fistfight (p14–p15).
- **Kilowog dies**: Hal incinerates him with a ring blast and leaves a charred body (p19–p20).
- **All Guardians except Ganthet die**: they give their last strength to Ganthet and are left as withered bodies (p23, p25–p26). Ganthet calls it "our ultimate sacrifice". Hal does not strike them on-panel.
- **Hal Jordan**: badly beaten (bloodied face, torn costume). He gives up his own ring, enters the Central Battery and emerges in a new costume, flying off apparently without a ring. The pages do not say where he goes.
- **Central Battery**: shown cracking and erupting (p23). The lantern looks toppled or broken on p25 [unclear how far it is destroyed; not stated in the lettering].
- **The Corps (recap only)**: Hal says he took their rings and left them enough to survive (p17). None of this is shown.
- **New ring-bearer**: Ganthet re-forms a ring from the crushed one and gives it to an unnamed young man on Earth, who becomes a Green Lantern (p27–p30).

## Ending / cliffhanger
On Earth, in an alley outside the Heretic Club, the unnamed young man has just put on Ganthet's ring and transformed into a Green Lantern costume while a homeless man watches. Ganthet is gone without explaining anything, and the young man says: "Oh man... I think my life just got a lot more complicated." The caption reads: "Next: Welcome to a new Emerald Era!" Hal's destination and plans after leaving Oa are left open.

## Easy to get wrong
- **Kyle Rayner is never named.** He is just a young man outside the Heretic Club. A synopsis that names him is using outside knowledge (which is accurate, but not in the lettering).
- **"Parallax" is never said.** Hal's new armored look appears without a name.
- **Hal does not kill the Guardians on-panel.** They give their last strength to Ganthet as a deliberate "ultimate sacrifice". The ComicVine summary's "he then takes the life of every Guardian" is an interpretation, not what the pages show.
- **Order of deaths:** Sinestro first (neck snap, by hand, not by ring), then Kilowog (ring blast, incinerated).
- **The Corps members are not killed in this issue.** The ring-taking and the trip to Oa (#48–49) are only mentioned in dialogue (Hal: "didn't kill the Corps, just took their rings"). Coast City's destruction is never mentioned by name. Hal's "I just wanted to have my life again" is the only hint of it.
- **Hal gives up his own ring** (p20) and later crushes a discarded ring (p25). Ganthet rebuilds a ring from those remains (p27). It is not stated outright that the crushed ring was the one Hal tossed. The new ring goes to the young man on Earth, not to Kilowog or any surviving Lantern.
- **Hal drops the extra rings** at Sinestro's goading early on (p05) but keeps one for the ring duel. The "no more rings" fistfight comes later.
- **Sinestro was released by the Guardians** from imprisonment in the Central Battery to stop Hal, with his freedom as the reward. He is not a surprise villain attack.
- **The young man saw the green light once before** ("just like the last time I saw it", footnote GL #48). That earlier sighting is a recap reference, not an event in this issue.
- **Not in this issue:** Kyle's costume redesign, Kyle's girlfriend Alex, Mongul, Ohm, and the Guy Gardner "Emerald Fallout" clash. These appear only as next-issue blurbs on the letters page or in later issues. Kyle's first costume here is a classic-style GL outfit.
- **Kilowog** is a Green Lantern who opposes Hal. He is not one of the Guardians and not "Kilowog the mentor".

## Reference synopsis
On Oa, Hal Jordan, wearing rings taken from other Green Lanterns, confronted Sinestro, whom the Guardians had freed from the Central Battery to stop him. Goaded by Sinestro, Hal dropped his extra rings and the two dueled while three Guardians watched. Their fight became a brutal fistfight, and Hal snapped Sinestro's neck. As Hal headed for the Central Battery, Kilowog attacked him and begged him to stop. Hal incinerated him instead. Hal took off his own ring, scorned the Guardians, and entered the battery. The Guardians poured their remaining strength into Ganthet and died. Hal emerged in a new armored costume, crushed a discarded ring underfoot, and flew off. Ganthet, now the last Guardian, re-forged the ring and went to Earth. There he gave it to a young man outside a nightclub and vanished. The young man put it on and became a Green Lantern. Hal's next move was unknown.
